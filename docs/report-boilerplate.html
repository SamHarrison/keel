1 · Executive summary

Three to five findings, each: claim → evidence chips → consequence. Not a tour of the sections.

  • F-1 · BLOCKER The claim, with its load-bearing nouns in chips (dere.euryale, skew.claro), and the consequence in the same breath.
  • F-2 · MEDIUM Second finding. Stable IDs (F-n, RT-n, K-n) are never renumbered after publication.
